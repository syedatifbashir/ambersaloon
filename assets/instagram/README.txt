INSTAGRAM GALLERY — AMBER'S SALON
================================
Official profile: https://www.instagram.com/ambersalon.official/?hl=en

Instagram did not allow direct retrieval of the public account's post photos
from this environment. Therefore, this folder contains no extracted Instagram
photos, and no photos from other salon accounts have been used as replacements.

To publish your real work on the gallery, export posts you own (with consent for
photographed clients) from Instagram/Meta or supply the originals, then put up
to six photos here named exactly:

  01.jpg, 02.jpg, 03.jpg, 04.jpg, 05.jpg, 06.jpg

You can also use PNG files, but adjust the filename in index.html's gallery
image src before upload. 4:5 portrait images work best. The gallery will
automatically show them; otherwise it displays illustrative stock content.

Do not upload photos of clients without appropriate permission.
